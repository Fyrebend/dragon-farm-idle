Expedition

Your party runs the selected expedition over and over. Expeditions never fail: a weak party is just slower, a strong one is faster. Clearing your newest zone with enough power unlocks the next. Drag a slime onto an identical one to merge (2 → next rank; two at +4 make the next tier), or tap one slime then tap where it goes.
