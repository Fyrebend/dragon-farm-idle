How it works
  • Your party runs the selected zone over and over. Runs never fail: a weak party is slower, a strong one faster (up to ×4).
  • Every run brings gold, and sometimes a slime (40% base; Better Nets adds 2% per level). Slimes only come from expeditions.
  • Drag a slime onto an identical one (same tier and rank) to merge it into the next rank. Two at +4 make the next tier.
  • Only teams add power. Clearing your newest zone once unlocks the next, at any power, but a badly under-powered team runs very slowly (check the run time before you commit a key). Switching zones mid-run refunds the key.
  • Zone 1 is free. Every other zone costs 1 key per run, and its keys drop in the zone below (each run has a % chance: higher when you are over-powered, low when under-powered; past 100% every full 100% is a guaranteed key, so 220% = 2 keys plus a 20% chance at a third). If a team runs out of keys, it moves down to the next zone it has keys for and stays down (all the way to Zone 1 if needed) until you pick a zone again. You can trade 6 keys of a zone for 1 key of the next zone.
  • Zone 5 unlocks Team B, a second team that runs its own zone at the same time (good for farming keys while Team A pushes). Equip best and Auto-Merge put your best slimes in Team A, the next best in Team B. Auto-Merge never merges two equipped slimes together: it only merges barn slimes, or a barn slime into an equipped one.
  • New zones can unlock new features. Zone 3 opens the Orders board: buyers want specific slimes for gold or Trail Rations (faster expeditions). Auto-Merge holds back slimes an order asks for.
  • Each slime shows its power (⚡). You can also tap a slime and then tap where it should go.
